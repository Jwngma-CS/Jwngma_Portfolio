Overview

The "Air Pollution Carrying Capacity of Delhi" report, prepared for the Delhi Pollution Control Committee in 2021, examines the limits of Delhi’s ability to absorb and manage air pollution without significant adverse impacts on public health and the environment. The report evaluates the current levels of air pollutants, such as PM2.5, PM10, NO2, and SO2, in relation to the city’s capacity to mitigate their effects. It provides insights into the sources of pollution, including vehicular emissions, industrial activities, and construction dust, and assesses the effectiveness of existing control measures. The findings and recommendations aim to inform policy decisions, enhance air quality management strategies, and promote sustainable urban planning to improve air quality and safeguard public health in Delhi.

Objectives

  • Assessing Adaptive Capacity
  • Evaluating Pollutant Sources and Impacts
  • Analyzing Existing Mitigation Measures
  • Recommendations for Improving Air Quality Management

The "Air Pollution Carrying Capacity of Delhi" report, published by the Delhi Pollution Control Committee in 2021, provides a comprehensive analysis of Delhi's capacity to manage and mitigate air pollution.

It assesses the current pollution levels, identifies major sources such as traffic, industry, and construction, and evaluates the effectiveness of existing pollution control measures. The report aims to establish a framework for understanding the limits of the city's air quality management and offers actionable recommendations to enhance pollution control strategies, improve air quality, and protect public health. By providing data-driven insights, the report supports informed policy-making and helps guide efforts towards achieving cleaner air and a healthier environment for Delhi's residents.
