Capstone Projects

Empowering students to tackle real-world environmental challenges through innovative planning exercises and sustainable solutions.

In the Environmental Planning Studio at the School of Planning and Architecture, New Delhi, we engage students in a range of hands-on exercises designed to address real-world environmental challenges. These studio exercises involve comprehensive site analysis, environmental impact assessment, and the development of sustainable land-use plans.

Students are encouraged to explore innovative solutions for issues such as urban sprawl, resource management, and climate resilience. The studio fosters critical thinking and equips students with the practical skills necessary for effective environmental planning, ensuring that they are well-prepared to contribute to sustainable development in diverse contexts.
