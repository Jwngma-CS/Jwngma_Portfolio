Project Details

Overview

As a consultant/project associate, I have contributed to the preparation of "Vision Document and Comprehensive Action Plan for Protection of Taj Mahal and its Environment," a project commissioned by U.P. Tourism in 2019. The project aimed to develop a strategic framework for safeguarding the iconic monument and its surrounding environment. It involved assessing the current environmental challenges, proposing sustainable practices, and recommending actions to preserve the site's historical and cultural integrity while enhancing the overall visitor experience.

About TTZ

The Taj Trapezium Zone (TTZ) is a trapezoid-shaped area that covers about 10,400 square kilometres and is spread over Agra, Firozabad, Mathura, Hathras and Etah districts of Uttar Pradesh and the Bharatpur district of Rajasthan.

On December 8, 2017, the top court had asked Uttar Pradesh government to prepare a comprehensive vision document and plan for the protection and preservation of Taj Mahal and the environment around it so that historic monument could be there for another 400 years and not just for a generation. Environmentalist M C Mehta, the petitioner in the case relating to protection of Taj Mahal, had told the apex court that green cover in the area has reduced and there were encroachments inside and outside the Yamuna flood plains.
