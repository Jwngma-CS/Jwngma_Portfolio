My Profession

Dedicated environmental planner, specializing in the study of land use zoning and its impact on natural resources. With a keen interest in data-driven analysis and sustainable development, I leverage advanced geospatial tools and databases to inform policy and management strategies.

My professional experience includes consulting on high-profile environmental projects, such as protecting cultural heritage sites, air quality management and managing urban wetlands.

Passionate about achieving climate goals, I am committed to making a positive impact through innovative solutions and comprehensive environmental planning.
