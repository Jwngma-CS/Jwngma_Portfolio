About Toolkit

The Environmental Information System (ENVIS) Green Skill Development Programme (GSDP) is a flagship initiative by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEF&CC) aimed at bridging the skill gap in the urban and environment sectors.

As part of this initiative, the School of Planning and Architecture (SPA) organized a specialized course to equip participants with the necessary technical and analytical skills in Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and Remote Sensing. The primary objectives of the ENVIS GSDP course were to:
1. Provide participants with hands-on training in GIS and Remote Sensing, focusing on practical applications relevant to environmental management and sustainable planning.
2. Equip students with the tools and knowledge required to analyze and address environmental challenges, thereby fostering sustainable development practices.
3. Develop a skilled workforce capable of implementing and supporting environmental initiatives across various sectors.
4. Align training outcomes with the broader objectives of the MoEF&CC to promote green skills and contribute to national environmental conservation efforts.
