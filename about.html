Who I Am

Dedicated environmental planner, specializing in the study of land use zoning and its impact on natural resources. With a keen interest in data-driven analysis and sustainable development, I leverage advanced geospatial tools and databases to inform policy and management strategies.

Hi there! I’m Jwngma, environmental planner with a passion for spatial planning and creating exceptional digital experiences. With over more than 5 years in the industry, I have skills in spatial planning, data analytics, designing apps and websites that are not only visually appealing but also functional and user-friendly. My professional experience includes consulting on high-profile environmental projects, such as protecting cultural heritage sites, air quality management and managing urban wetlands.

Passionate about achieving climate goals, I am committed to making a positive impact through innovative solutions and comprehensive environmental planning. I also specialize in crafting bespoke websites using the latest technologies and design trends, including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and popular content management systems like WordPress, WIX, and Shopify.
