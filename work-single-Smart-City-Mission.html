Project Details

Overview

The Smart City Mission Project Assessment was an initiative aimed at evaluating the progress and effectiveness of India's Smart Cities Mission, a flagship program launched by the Government of India. The project involved a comprehensive assessment of various smart city initiatives across the country, focusing on key areas such as infrastructure development, digitalization, and citizen engagement.

Objectives

  • Assessment of Riverfront Projects under SCM
  • Assessment of Lakefront Projects under SCM
  • Assessment of Beachfront Projects under SCM
  • Evaluating Environmental Impacts of the Projects
  • Analyzing Project Components
  • Reporting the status of SCM Projects

About Project

"Evaluating Urban Innovation: Comprehensive Assessment of India's Smart City Mission"

The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs spearheaded this assessment to ensure that the cities were progressing towards becoming more sustainable, efficient, and livable. The evaluation process included analyzing the implementation of smart solutions like intelligent transportation systems, smart grids, and public safety improvements. It also assessed the cities' efforts in promoting e-governance, enhancing public services, and improving the quality of life for residents.

The project's findings provided valuable insights into the successes and challenges faced by the smart cities, offering recommendations for future improvements. This assessment was crucial in guiding policy decisions and ensuring that the Smart Cities Mission aligned with India's broader goals of urban development and sustainable growth.
