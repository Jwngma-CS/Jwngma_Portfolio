## Professional Focus

My primary areas of interest and practice include:

- **Urban and Environmental Planning:** sustainable urban development, land-use planning, environmental management, and climate-responsive planning.
- **Geographic Information Systems (GIS):** spatial database development, geospatial processing, mapping, and spatial analysis.
- **Remote Sensing:** earth observation, land-use and land-cover analysis, and environmental monitoring.
- **Urban Environmental Research:** air quality, urban heat, blue-green infrastructure, urban wetlands, water bodies, and ecosystem services.
- **Spatial and Data Analytics:** translating complex datasets into meaningful insights for planning, research, and decision-making.
- **Visual Communication:** communicating planning evidence through maps, dashboards, diagrams, reports, and data visualisations.

## Tools & Technologies

My work involves a combination of geospatial, analytical, visualisation, and digital tools, including:

- **GIS & Remote Sensing:** ArcGIS, QGIS, Google Earth Engine
- **Data Analysis & Visualisation:** Microsoft Excel and other data visualisation and analytics tools
- **Web & Digital:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and web-based presentation of information

The tools used vary by project and are selected according to the research question, data, and intended planning outcome.

## Background

I hold a **Master's degree in Environmental Planning** and a **Bachelor's degree in Physical Planning** from the School of Planning and Architecture, New Delhi. My professional and academic experience includes research, consultancy, teaching, and collaborative planning assignments.

I have contributed to work involving urban environmental assessment, air pollution and carrying-capacity studies, water body and wetland management, smart city project assessment, hill area development, and site-level environmental improvement. I also engage with teaching and mentoring in environmental planning, geoinformatics, and research methodology.

## Purpose of This Portfolio

This portfolio is intended as a structured record of my professional journey and selected work. It aims to:

- Present planning and research work in an accessible, visual format.
- Demonstrate how GIS and spatial analytics can support evidence-based planning.
- Document projects, methods, outputs, and areas of continuing interest.
- Share knowledge and practical learning resources with students and early-career professionals.
- Create opportunities for professional exchange, research, and collaboration.
