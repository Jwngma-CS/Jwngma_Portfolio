Project Details

Overview

In 2020, as a consultant/project associate, I was involved in the development of the "Urban Wetlands/Water Bodies Management Guideline - A Toolkit for Local Stakeholders" for the National Mission for Clean Ganga. This project aimed to create a comprehensive toolkit to guide local stakeholders in the effective management and conservation of urban wetlands and water bodies. The guidelines focused on sustainable practices, ecological restoration, and community involvement, with the overarching goal of protecting these vital ecosystems and enhancing urban water quality and biodiversity.

About Toolkit

The "Urban Wetlands/Water Bodies Management Guideline - A Toolkit for Local Stakeholders," developed in 2020 for the National Mission for Clean Ganga, serves as a comprehensive resource for managing and conserving urban wetlands and water bodies.

This toolkit provides practical guidelines and best practices aimed at local stakeholders, including municipal authorities, community groups, and environmental organizations. It emphasizes the importance of sustainable management, ecological restoration, and biodiversity conservation. The document addresses challenges such as pollution, encroachment, and habitat degradation, offering strategies to improve water quality, enhance urban resilience, and support ecosystem services. By fostering community engagement and awareness, the toolkit aims to empower stakeholders to play a proactive role in the stewardship of these critical urban ecosystems, ultimately contributing to the overall health and sustainability of the Ganga River basin.
